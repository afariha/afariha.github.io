Anna Fariha

Assistant Professor, Kahlert School of Computing, University of Utah

I am an Assistant Professor at Kahlert School of Computing, the University of Utah. I co-lead the Data Management Research Center for Human-centered, Efficient, and Scalable Systems. I was a researcher at Microsoft PROSE Research and Engineering team. I obtained my Ph.D. from Manning College of Information and Computer Sciences (CICS), University of Massachusetts, Amherst under the supervision of Prof. Alexandra Meliou.

I build human-centered data systems that make working with data easier for everyone, from novices to experienced data scientists. My recent work focuses on recommendation: systems that suggest useful data summaries and data-wrangling steps, so users do not have to start from scratch. I also build tools that let people interact with data more naturally, for example, by giving examples instead of writing queries, and that help them understand their data and systems. I enjoy designing new algorithms and turning theory into practical tools.
